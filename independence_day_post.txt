In 1947, independence was about political sovereignty.

In 2026, independence is about technological self-reliance — the autonomy to build, innovate, and solve our own foundational problems from first principles.

For decades, India was primarily recognized for servicing global technology stacks.

Today, we are architecting them.

When you look at what Indian builders, engineers, and product teams are creating right now:
• We are designing Digital Public Infrastructure that processes billions of transactions with near-zero latency.
• We are building AI systems that work seamlessly across 22+ languages and low-bandwidth environments for 1.4 billion people.
• We are shifting from execution-only workflows to product-led innovation and agentic systems built for real-world complexity.

True self-reliance in tech doesn't come from closing borders or replicating what already exists. 

It comes from building the muscle to solve complex, ground-level problems at massive scale — and then exporting those resilient solutions to the rest of the world.

As product managers, developers, and founders, our real opportunity with AI and technology isn't chasing hype cycles. 

It is using technology as a democratizing force: removing friction, expanding access to knowledge, and empowering the next generation of builders.

The tools of creation have never been more accessible. The responsibility to build meaningful things has never been higher.

Wishing everyone a thoughtful, proud, and inspired 80th Independence Day! 🇮🇳
