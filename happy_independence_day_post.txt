Today, as we celebrate India's freedom and incredible journey, I can’t help but reflect on what "Independence" means for our generation of builders, creators, and innovators.

In 1947, our freedom fighters gave us the gift of sovereignty.
In 2026, our responsibility is building technological and economic self-reliance.

Over the past few years, the energy in the Indian tech ecosystem has shifted in a profound way:
✨ We are no longer just building for global clients — we are architecting world-class AI, SaaS, and product-first solutions right from India.
✨ We built the world's most resilient Digital Public Infrastructure (UPI, ONDC, Aadhaar) processing billions of transactions effortlessly.
✨ We are building multilingual AI models and grassroots tech that democratize access to education, healthcare, and opportunity for 1.4 billion people.

True freedom in the digital age means:
• The autonomy to solve our own country’s unique challenges from first principles.
• The confidence to build products that set global benchmarks in scale and efficiency.
• The mindset shift from "How do we execute this task?" to "How do we shape the future of technology?"

To all the developers, product managers, designers, founders, and students working tirelessly to build the future of India — your curiosity and ambition are driving the next chapter of our nation's story.

Let's continue to build fearlessly, innovate with empathy, and take Indian tech to the global stage. 🚀
